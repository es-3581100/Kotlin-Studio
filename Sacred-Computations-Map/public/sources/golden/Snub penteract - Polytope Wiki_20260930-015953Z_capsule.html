The **snub penteract** or **snan** is a convex [isogonal](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Isogonal_polytope) [polyteron](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Polyteron) that consists of 10 [snub tesseracts](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Snub_tesseract), 32 [snub pentachora](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Snub_pentachoron), 40 [snub cubic antiprisms](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Snub_cubic_antiprism), 80 [snub tetrahedral antiprisms](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Snub_tetrahedral_antiprism), 80 [triangular-square duoantiprisms](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Triangular-square_duoantiprism), and 1920 irregular [pentachora](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Pentachoron). 5 pentachora and 1 of each of the other facet types join at each vertex. It can be obtained through the process of [alternating](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Alternation) the [great cellipenteractitriacontaditeron](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Great_cellipenteractitriacontaditeron). However, it cannot be made uniform.

Using the ratio method, the lowest possible ratio between the longest and shortest edges is 1:          24 + 9   2    23        {\displaystyle {\displaystyle {\sqrt {\frac {24+9{\sqrt {2}}}{23}}}}}   ≈ 1:1.26367.

The **snub penteract** or **snan** is a convex [isogonal](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Isogonal_polytope) [polyteron](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Polyteron) that consists of 10 [snub tesseracts](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Snub_tesseract), 32 [snub pentachora](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Snub_pentachoron), 40 [snub cubic antiprisms](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Snub_cubic_antiprism), 80 [snub tetrahedral antiprisms](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Snub_tetrahedral_antiprism), 80 [triangular-square duoantiprisms](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Triangular-square_duoantiprism), and 1920 irregular [pentachora](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Pentachoron). 5 pentachora and 1 of each of the other facet types join at each vertex. It can be obtained through the process of [alternating](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Alternation) the [great cellipenteractitriacontaditeron](https://polytope.miraheze.org/wiki/Great_cellipenteractitriacontaditeron). However, it cannot be made uniform.

Using the ratio method, the lowest possible ratio between the longest and shortest edges is 1:          24 + 9   2    23        {\displaystyle {\displaystyle {\sqrt {\frac {24+9{\sqrt {2}}}{23}}}}}   ≈ 1:1.26367.
